## Receiver contract and limitations

QSE is **reference-assisted**: its receiver uses clean NF4 values and reference residuals at carrier positions. Do not describe it as blind or artifact-only extraction. The saved artifact is a research artifact (`multi_tensor_packed_nf4_experiment_artifact_not_hf_checkpoint`), not a full Hugging Face checkpoint.

This single run establishes exact recovery for this model/corpus/configuration only. It does **not** establish:
- low or negligible detectability;
- utility preservation;
- robustness to pruning, fine-tuning, or re-quantization;
- generalization across seeds, corpora, or models.

## Freeze policy
